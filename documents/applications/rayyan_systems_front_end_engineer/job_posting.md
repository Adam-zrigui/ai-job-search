 Role Overview Rayyan is an AI SaaS startup developing a research collaboration platform with an AI-assisted workflow that helps accelerate searching, extracting, and synthesizing relevant information from research studies to inform evidence-based decision making, including evidence-based medicine.

 As a Frontend Software Engineer at Rayyan Systems Inc., you will design, develop, and implement new and next-generation features with a focus on frontend technologies. You'll ship end-to-end product experiences from onboarding and core workflows to complex data interactions and AI-powered features. Working within a collaborative environment, you'll partner with product managers, designers, and backend engineers to transform user needs into elegant, functional software.

 This is a growth role where you'll expand your skills in SaaS product development, AI-first interfaces, and complex frontend architecture while contributing meaningfully from day one.

 What We're Looking For You're excited about building products that solve real problems in research and evidence-based decision making. You understand that great frontend engineering is about creating experiences that are fast, usable, and delightful. You're hungry to learn, especially about AI-powered interfaces and modern SaaS architecture. You're comfortable with ambiguity and eager to grow your skills while making an immediate impact.

- Contribute to and maintain shared component libraries and design-system implementations that ensure consistency across the product

 AI-First Experience Engineering

- Build intuitive interfaces for AI interactions, automated workflows, and intelligent recommendations

 Collaboration & Growth

- Write clean, tested code and help improve frontend quality, performance, and developer experience



- Stay current with frontend best practices, SaaS UI patterns, and AI interface trends
